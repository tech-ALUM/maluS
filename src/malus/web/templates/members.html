What each role can do

  • owner Owns the document: freezes the baseline, answers findings (dispose / reply / resolution), implements accepted ones. Does not add comments and can never verify or close a finding.
  • reviewer Reviews the document: adds comments in their own copy, submits it, and — closure authority — verifies their own findings once addressed.
  • moderator Steers the process: harvest/triage and verify/reopen on a reviewer's behalf. Authors no comments, takes no dispositions.

One role per person per review — the formal flow keeps document author and reviewers separate. A global admin passes every gate (but an AI principal never verifies or commits, regardless of role).
